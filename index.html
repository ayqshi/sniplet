The same uncluttered sniplit reshapes itself — thumb-first on your phone, a calm sidebar on desktop, a ten-foot view on TV.

Lyrics

Every word, on the beat.

sniplit listens to the vocals in whatever is playing and lines lyrics up word by word — then checks its own work. Off by a hair? Tap the line you hear.

  • Karaoke fill & smooth scroll
  • Drag to browse, tap to jump
  • Share any lines as a card
Studio

Bend any track. Keep it.

Speed and pitch move independently. Ten-band EQ, bass from −30 to +40 dB, space and 8D, and vocals removed or isolated — then export the result.

  • Slowed + reverb, sped up, nightcore in a tap
  • Loop any section on the waveform
  • WAV, AIFF, Opus, AAC, raw PCM
Friends

Listen together, directly.

Add friends with a link — no accounts. See who’s active and what they’re playing, open their playlists, and tap their daily recap. It all travels device-to-device, encrypted.

  • Live presence & now playing
  • Daily recap stories · year in review every New Year
  • Send a song straight to a friend
Discovery

The songs that get you hooked.

Your feed bends to what you like, follow and replay. Every artist opens into a Vault of deep cuts and rarities — and every song shows once, with all its versions inside.

  • Plays on the lock screen and offline
